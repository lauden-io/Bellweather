# Benchmark report: 1 week

**Period:** 10 November 2026 to 17 November 2026 (UTC) · **Lead times:** next hours, day 1, 2 and 3 · **Generated:** 18 November 2026 05:16

## How to read this

Every number says how far a forecast was from what an official weather station then measured, averaged over every hour: lower is closer to reality. All are scored on the same hours with the same measurements; nobody saw the answer in advance. Details: [What "more accurate" means](../benchmark.md#what-more-accurate-means).

Competitors are listed by how many Home Assistant installations use them (Home Assistant analytics, 8 November 2026); ECMWF IFS is the weather model most providers build on, shown as a reference.

> [!NOTE]
> One week is too short to judge: differences of a few hundredths are within chance.

**Captures:** 38 of 42 made (missed: 11 Nov 11:00, 11 Nov 15:00, 16 Nov 19:00, 17 Nov 03:00 UTC). A missed capture is never made up later, because a later one would see newer forecasts; every provider misses the same ones, so the comparison stays fair, on fewer hours. Station reports missed during a gap are fetched again automatically while the services still keep them.

## Notes

- **Bellweather** runs at each address as in Home Assistant, with the airport archive on (nearest airport within 60 km other than the scoring station). The **control** is the same models' plain mean.
- **OpenWeather** gives 3-hourly values; when it takes part, only every third hour can be paired. Its dew point is computed from its temperature and humidity.
- **Wet** compares 3-hour blocks (00, 03, … UTC): forecast wet when its amount for the block is at least 0.1 mm, observed wet when any report in the block had precipitation. CSI = hits / (hits + misses + false alarms), 1 is perfect. Brier: the highest rain probability of the block against what happened, 0 is perfect.
- **Daily high and low** use local mean time days (longitude / 15 hours) and the latest forecast made at least 6 hours before the day began that every compared provider answered.
- **Pressure** is sea-level pressure. An airport that reports only QNH is converted with its official height and the mean of its temperature now and 12 hours earlier. Scored on captures from 16 Nov 11:00 UTC on: before, QNH was taken as it is ([Changes](../benchmark.md#changes)).
- Data: E-SOH and aviationweather.gov (observations); Open-Meteo.com, MET Norway, DWD (CC BY 4.0); OpenWeather (ODbL).

Addresses, stations and the method: [docs/benchmark.md](../benchmark.md) and `benchmark/points.json`.
